# Faceted Depths

## Scope and Mode

Experience surface for a four-level keyboard-controlled dungeon run in Godot. The player explores distinct connected maps, fights themed enemies, collects three shards per level, and reaches each gate.

## Direction Contract

THESIS: The dungeon is a living cut gemstone; the default brown-stone room is replaced by mineral strata, refracted color, and light that responds to play.

OWN-WORLD: Deep ink frames jewel-toned stone planes, with amber for action, cyan for safety, and oxblood for danger. Each depth shifts the mineral palette and enemy silhouette while preserving those semantic signals. Phosphor raise: whole-pixel camera steps and strict state colors. Glazier raise: one saturated mineral role per room.

STORY: The player descends through four authored depths—Faceted Depths, Mossglass Cistern, Ember Vault, and Starfall Reliquary—recovering three light shards before each gate seals, with enemies, pickups, and exit visible at a glance.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The warrior begins near the lower-left of a full-room isometric field; objective, health, shards, and controls occupy restrained edge plaques without obscuring play.

FORM: Faceted Depths, grounded candidate 3, seed key b277060b.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Decisions

- Phosphor Catacomb: declined; kept whole-pixel camera motion and strict state color.
- Leaded Glass Vault: competitive; kept one saturated mineral role per room.
- Moonlit Paper Theatre: declined; kept crisp foreground silhouette separation.
