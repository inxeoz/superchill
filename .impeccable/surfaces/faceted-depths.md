# Faceted Depths

## Scope and Mode

Experience surface for a five-entry keyboard-controlled run in Godot. A launch-time level selector offers the open-air River Run and four authored dungeon depths; River Run gathers and crafts bottles before a river crossing, while each depth explores a connected map, fights themed enemies, collects three shards, and reaches the gate.

## Direction Contract

THESIS: The dungeon is a living cut gemstone; the default brown-stone room is replaced by mineral strata, refracted color, and light that responds to play.

OWN-WORLD: Deep ink frames jewel-toned stone planes, with amber for action, cyan for safety, and oxblood for danger. Each depth shifts the mineral palette and enemy silhouette while preserving those semantic signals. Phosphor raise: whole-pixel camera steps and strict state colors. Glazier raise: one saturated mineral role per room.

STORY: The player begins in open daylight, searches a leaf-filled dustbin and three alternate bottle sources, selects those elements at a craft table, weaves an eight-bottle life jacket, and crosses to the dungeon before descending through four authored depths that each hide three light shards and themed enemies.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The warrior begins on a grassy near bank beneath an open faceted sky; bottle sources, the diagonal river, bottle/jacket state, and restrained controls remain readable without obscuring the route.

FORM: Faceted Depths, grounded candidate 3, seed key b277060b.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
