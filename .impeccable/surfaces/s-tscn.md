# Faceted Depths

## Scope and Mode

Experience surface for a compact keyboard-controlled dungeon run in Godot. The player explores, fights, collects three shards, and reaches the gate.

## Direction Contract

THESIS: The dungeon is a living cut gemstone; the default brown-stone room is replaced by mineral strata, refracted color, and light that responds to play.

OWN-WORLD: Deep ink frames jewel-toned stone planes, with amber for action, cyan for safety, and oxblood for danger. Phosphor raise: whole-pixel camera steps and strict state colors. Glazier raise: one saturated mineral role per room.

STORY: The player is a lone warrior recovering the three light shards before the gate seals, with enemies, pickups, and exit visible at a glance.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The warrior begins near the lower-left of a full-room isometric field; objective, health, shards, and controls occupy restrained edge plaques without obscuring play.

FORM: Faceted Depths, grounded candidate 3, seed key b277060b.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Decisions

- Leaded Glass Vault: competitive; kept one saturated mineral role per room.
