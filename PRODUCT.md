# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Players who want a short, immediately playable isometric dungeon crawl using keyboard controls.

## Product Purpose

Deliver a compact five-level Godot run: an open-sky River Run where the player gathers empty bottles, weaves a flotation jacket, and crosses a river, followed by four isometric dungeon depths to explore, fight, and escape. Success means a satisfying run can be completed in a few minutes.

## Positioning

A fast, readable adventure built around immediate keyboard movement, a tactile survival opening, close-range combat, and a clear exit objective.

## Operating Context

Godot 4.7, desktop-first with a web-export-friendly implementation, rendered at a 1280×720 landscape viewport. The primary controls are WASD or arrow keys.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The run includes a deterministic combat-free Level 0 plus four connected dungeon depths, a launch-time level selector, bottle gathering, bottle-jacket crafting, jacket-gated river crossing, themed enemy behavior, three-shard objectives, and level exits. Desktop and web targets must share the same gameplay code. Only supplied assets with known redistribution terms may ship; other packs remain blocked pending license confirmation.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains an initial Godot scene, the Phantom Camera addon, and RPG asset packs under `game-res/`. The Tiny Questers warrior pack permits use in personal and commercial games but prohibits standalone redistribution; its license is stored beside the art. License evidence for the other supplied packs has not been found.

## Product Principles

- Start playing in seconds.
- Keep movement and combat immediately legible.
- Make progress visible within every run and between levels.
- Use supplied art only within its confirmed license.
- Keep desktop and web behavior aligned.
